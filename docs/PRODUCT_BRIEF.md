# ASTERA Product Brief

## One-line promise

ASTERA is a private estate operations command center that turns fragmented incidents, vendors, approvals, assets, and audit records into one calm, accountable workflow.

## The problem

High-value residences, villas, and family offices are often managed through chat threads, spreadsheets, phone calls, and individual memory. That creates avoidable risk:

- urgent incidents arrive without consistent severity or context;
- vendor quotes are difficult to compare and approve;
- principals are interrupted for operational details but still lack oversight;
- maintenance history and accountability disappear when staff changes;
- sensitive household information is copied across tools with unclear access.

## Initial customer

The initial wedge is the professional estate manager responsible for two to ten premium properties in Indonesia. The economic buyer is the principal, family office, or hospitality operator. Staff and trusted vendors participate with limited access.

## Golden workflow

1. A steward reports a water leak at a Bali villa using text, photo, or voice.
2. ASTERA structures the report, proposes severity, identifies the affected asset, and recommends containment steps.
3. The estate manager compares normalized vendor quotes and selects a recommendation.
4. The principal receives a concise approval request with amount, risk, and evidence.
5. Approval dispatches the vendor and writes a timestamped audit event.
6. Resolution evidence closes the incident and enriches the asset history.

## Value proposition

- **Principals:** oversight without operational noise.
- **Estate managers:** faster decisions with evidence and accountability.
- **Staff:** one clear place to report and follow work.
- **Vendors:** structured briefs, approval status, and proof-of-completion requirements.

## Product principles

- **Calm by default:** show exceptions and decisions, not a wall of telemetry.
- **Human authorization:** AI may summarize and recommend; accountable people approve spending and dispatch.
- **Evidence before action:** costs, photos, scope, and risk remain attached to the decision.
- **Least privilege:** every role sees only the estates and records needed for its job.
- **Auditability:** important state changes are append-only events in production.
- **Delight with restraint:** anime-inspired 3D art creates memorability; operational surfaces stay professional and legible.

## Prototype scope

The contest build proves the full interaction loop with synthetic data: estate overview, incident intake, AI-assisted triage, quote comparison, approval, dispatch plan, notifications, search, vendor context, and audit trail. It does not claim to connect to real homes, payment systems, or vendors yet.

## Business model hypothesis

Subscription per estate with usage bands for active assets, collaborators, and automation volume. A higher tier can add family-office reporting, integrations, dedicated onboarding, and service-level commitments. Vendor referral fees are intentionally excluded from the first model to preserve recommendation neutrality.

## Success measures

- median time from incident report to containment decision;
- percentage of spending decisions with complete evidence;
- planned versus emergency maintenance ratio;
- unresolved high-severity incidents past SLA;
- principal interruptions per property per month;
- asset records with complete maintenance history.

## Non-goals for the first release

- replacing emergency services;
- autonomous payments or vendor dispatch without configured approval;
- surveillance or biometric monitoring;
- a public vendor marketplace;
- storing household secrets that are not required for operations.
